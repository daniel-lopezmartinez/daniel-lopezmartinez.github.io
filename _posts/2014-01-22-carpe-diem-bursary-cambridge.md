In 2013, I was awarded the **Carpe Diem Enterprise Trust Bursary** to support my studies in the [Master's in Bioscience Enterprise (MBE)](https://www.postgraduate.study.cam.ac.uk/courses/directory/cbchmpbie) programme at the University of Cambridge.

### About the Award

The Carpe Diem Enterprise Trust bursaries were established by [Dr Darrin Disley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darrin_Disley), a serial biotechnology entrepreneur and at the time CEO of [Horizon Discovery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horizon_Discovery). The bursaries are awarded annually to a small number of MBE students to support them pursuing this programme, which bridges science, business, and entrepreneurship at Cambridge.
