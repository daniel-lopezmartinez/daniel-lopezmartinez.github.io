In 2013, I was awarded the **ACGI Medal for Excellence** by the Faculty of Engineering at Imperial College London upon completing my M.Eng. in Biomedical Engineering with First Class Honours.

### About the Award

The ACGI (Associate of the City and Guilds of London Institute) Medal for Excellence is awarded every year to a single graduating student in the Faculty of Engineering who has demonstrated outstanding academic achievement throughout their degree. The award recognises exceptional performance across the full programme of study.

The ACGI designation connects to Imperial's historical roots as the City and Guilds College — one of the founding colleges of Imperial College London.
