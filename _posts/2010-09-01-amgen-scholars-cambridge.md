In the summer of 2010, I was selected for the [Amgen Scholars Programme](https://amgenscholars.com/) to conduct research at the [Wolfson Brain Imaging Centre](https://www.wbic.cam.ac.uk/), University of Cambridge, under the supervision of Adrian Carpenter and Steve Sawiak. The programme had an acceptance rate of approximately 6% that year.

My research focused on evaluating the detection limits of automated MRI morphometry for identifying structural brain changes in rodent models of neurological disease. I presented this work as a poster at the Amgen Scholars European Symposium 2010, held at the University of Cambridge.
