During my PhD at MIT, [Tim Cook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tim_Cook) visited my research group, the [Affective Computing group](https://www.media.mit.edu/groups/affective-computing/overview/) at the [MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/). Our team presented our research showcasing work on AI, emotion recognition, and physiological computing.
