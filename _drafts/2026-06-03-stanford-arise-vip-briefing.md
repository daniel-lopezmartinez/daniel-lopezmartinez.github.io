I participated in the [ARISE](https://arise-ai.org/) VIP briefing in Palo Alto, where leaders across health AI, clinical medicine, research, and industry discussed the future of medical AI evaluation.

ARISE — the AI Research and Science Evaluation Healthcare Network — is doing some of the most important work in the field: building rigorous, realistic, and independent benchmarks for clinical AI. Its [Medical AI Superintelligence Test](https://arise-ai.org/mast), or [MAST](https://arise-ai.org/mast), is especially relevant because it moves beyond traditional medical knowledge benchmarks and toward the harder question of whether AI systems can perform safely and reliably in realistic clinical settings.
